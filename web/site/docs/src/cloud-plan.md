# Cloud plan

The komando command line is free and open source, and the Komando apps for Mac, iPhone and iPad will be free too. This page is about the paid plan that comes later. It is not live yet.

## What is free now

- Every command, as text or JSON, on your own machine.
- Terminal tabs, database clients and port forwarding in the apps.
- One-click fixes, the agent and the menu bar in the apps.
- No account and no trial.

## What the plan is meant to add

- Sync between your devices.
- Teams.
- Built-in AI, with no API key of your own.
- Approving changes from your phone.

## Where it stands

Nothing can be bought yet. The price and the way billing works are not set. This page will say so when the plan is ready, and what it costs.
